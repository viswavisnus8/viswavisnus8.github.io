How to Use AI for a Better Job Search

AI is most useful as an editor, researcher and practice partner—not as a tool to invent experience. This guide shows a repeatable process for job seekers.

1. Start with a role shortlist

Pick two or three related job titles instead of applying randomly. Compare five real job advertisements and record the skills repeated most often. Group them into essential skills, useful skills and requirements you do not yet meet.

Example shortlist

Operations Coordinator · Retail Supervisor · Business Development Associate

If three of five roles mention reporting, build a concrete example of your spreadsheet or dashboard work.

2. Build an evidence bank

Write down real achievements from studies, part-time work, volunteering or projects. Use a simple structure: action + context + measurable or observable result. If you do not have a reliable number, describe the outcome accurately without inventing one.

  • Action: redesigned a stock-check procedure
  • Context: during busy weekend shifts
  • Result: fewer missed checks and clearer handovers

3. Use AI to review—not fabricate—your CV

Give the model a job description and your actual CV. Ask it to identify gaps, unclear wording and transferable experience. Review every proposed change yourself.

Copyable prompt

“Compare this job description with my experience. Identify the five strongest genuine matches, the three biggest gaps and clearer bullet points. Do not invent degrees, duties, employers, dates, metrics or language fluency. Ask questions if facts are missing.”

4. Organise your applications

Keep one row for each application in a spreadsheet. This prevents repeat applications and makes follow-up more manageable.

5. Prepare for interviews

Write three truthful STAR stories covering a challenge, a team achievement and a mistake you learned from. Practise speaking your answer aloud. Ask AI to evaluate clarity but never memorise a fictional story.

Practice prompt

“Act as an interviewer for a junior operations role. Ask one question at a time. After my answer, assess whether I explained Situation, Task, Action and Result, and suggest one specific improvement.”

6. Final checklist

  • The CV reflects real qualifications and dates.
  • Each application targets a specific job.
  • AI suggestions were checked for accuracy.
  • No private identification documents were pasted into public tools.
  • Application dates and follow-ups are tracked.
