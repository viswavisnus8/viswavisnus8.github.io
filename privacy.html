Privacy Policy

Application: Viswa Media Automation

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Viswa Media Automation is an automation application operated by Viswanathan Selvaraj. It supports AI-assisted content production and authorized publishing to connected platforms, including YouTube.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, uses, and protects information.

1. Information Accessed

When the account owner authorizes access through Google OAuth, the application may access the permissions granted for YouTube video publishing. This may include the ability to upload videos and retrieve information associated with those uploads.

The application does not require Google account passwords.

2. How Information Is Used

Authorized access is used to:

  • Authenticate the connected YouTube account.
  • Upload videos prepared by the automation workflow.
  • Receive upload responses and record workflow status.
  • Troubleshoot publishing errors.

Google account information is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

3. Credentials and Security

The application uses OAuth authorization instead of collecting Google passwords. Publishing credentials are configured for automated workflows and should be stored in restricted secret storage, such as GitHub Actions Secrets.

Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account owner. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google and YouTube APIs. Publishing workflows may also use GitHub Actions and other configured content-production services. Those services process relevant information under their own applicable terms and policies.

5. Data Retention

Workflow logs and publishing records may be retained to monitor automation performance and troubleshoot errors. Their retention depends on the configured storage services and workflow settings.

6. Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account Connections .

Revoking authorization prevents further API access using the revoked credentials. Content already published to YouTube is not automatically deleted.

7. Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including applicable Limited Use requirements.

8. Free Reading Library

The public Reading Library offers educational PDF files on this website. Visitors can read and download these guides without signing in, submitting an email address or providing payment information. The library does not currently collect reader-submitted personal information.

The files are served through the website hosting provider, which may process basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and maintain security, according to its own policies.

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate visitor counts, page views, referring sources, and website performance. The analytics service is designed to operate without analytics cookies or cross-site visitor profiles. Cloudflare processes technical request data to provide this service under its privacy policy. We do not use this analytics service to collect names, email addresses, or payment details from Reading Library visitors. Browser privacy controls may prevent analytics collection.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality, service integrations, or information-handling practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the professional channels available on viswavisnus.com .
