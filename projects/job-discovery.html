PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making job discovery more systematic

Searching across job boards, reviewing descriptions, identifying relevant opportunities, and maintaining an application tracker can require substantial repetitive manual effort.

This project explores how automation and AI-assisted analysis can simplify job research while keeping applications and final decisions under human control.

🔎

Opportunity Discovery

Collect opportunities from supported job APIs and authorized public sources.

🧠

AI Role Matching

Assess job descriptions against professional skills, experience, and role requirements.

📋

Structured Tracking

Organize job details and application statuses in a central tracking spreadsheet.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How the automation works

The proposed architecture connects job discovery, data preparation, AI-assisted evaluation, duplicate checking, and application tracking.

01 — Scheduled Trigger

Starts job discovery at a configured interval.

↓
02 — Job Sources & APIs

Retrieves job titles, employers, locations, descriptions, and application links.

↓
03 — Data Normalization

Structures source data into a consistent format for comparison and filtering.

↓
04 — AI-Assisted Matching

Produces a relevance score and short explanation based on role requirements.

↓
05 — Duplicate Checking

Intended to avoid repeated opportunities in the application tracker.

↓
06 — Google Sheets Tracker

Stores new job opportunities with relevant details and tracking status.

↓
07 — Human Review

Reviews shortlisted opportunities before taking any application action.

Development note: This diagram represents the intended end-to-end architecture. Individual components are being tested and improved; it is not a claim that every stage is currently running unattended.

ROADMAP

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Designed around human control

AI-generated match scores are decision-support estimates rather than guarantees of hiring suitability or application success.

Job applications are not submitted automatically without user review and approval.

Private API credentials, personal application records, and internal workflow configurations are not published on this website.
