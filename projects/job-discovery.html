01 / Project Overview

Making job discovery
more systematic.

The Challenge

Searching different career platforms, reading job descriptions, identifying skill requirements, and tracking applications creates repetitive manual work.

Without a centralized system, comparing opportunities and monitoring application progress becomes difficult.

The Solution

Build a structured workflow that collects job information, analyzes relevance, identifies possible skill gaps, and stores the results in Google Sheets.

Each opportunity can then be reviewed, prioritized, and tracked without automatically submitting an application.

03 / System Architecture

How the
automation works.

The project connects job discovery, information processing, AI-assisted matching, and application tracking.

01

Job Discovery

Retrieve opportunities from supported job APIs and authorized sources.

02

Data Preparation

Normalize job titles, companies, locations, descriptions, and URLs.

03

AI Matching

Compare role requirements with professional skills and experience.

04

Skill-Gap Analysis

Highlight relevant requirements and possible missing skills.

05

Tracker Updates

Record opportunities, match scores, application links, and related notes in Google Sheets.

06

Human Review

Evaluate opportunities and decide which applications to pursue.

07 / Responsible Automation

AI-assisted discovery.
Human-controlled decisions.

The purpose of the system is to support job research and prioritization. Match scores are estimates that require human interpretation.

  • Application decisions remain under human control
  • No automatic applications without approval
  • Internal application records are not publicly shared
  • API credentials and private configurations remain protected
  • Historical examples are not represented as live vacancies
