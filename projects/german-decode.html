Project Overview

German Decode is an experimental content automation project focused on creating educational short-form videos using a repeatable production pipeline.

The goal is to reduce repetitive manual production tasks while maintaining useful scripts, clear narration, relevant visuals, and consistent video quality.

How the Automation Works

The active automation connects AI-assisted content generation, narration, vertical video rendering, and scheduled YouTube publishing. Visual quality and subtitle improvements remain ongoing.
