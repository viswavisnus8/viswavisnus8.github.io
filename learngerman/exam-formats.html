German exam formats

Compare DTZ, telc Deutsch and Goethe-Zertifikat for adult learners at A1–B1. Times are for the standard exam format. Check the exact exam name on your booking before choosing a sample paper.

DTZ · Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer

DTZ is the A2–B1 language test for Germany’s integration courses. For an overall B1 result, you need B1 in Sprechen and in either Hören/Lesen or Schreiben, and at least A2 in the remaining scored area.

A2–B1

Written
Hören ≈25 min · Lesen 45 min · Schreiben 30 min

Sprechen
≈16 min, usually in pairs, with no preparation time.

Listening and reading test everyday information. Choose one of two writing tasks and cover four points. Speaking includes introducing yourself, talking about an image and your experience, and planning something together.

Official DTZ format, practice sets and audio

telc Deutsch · General German

These are the single-level general German exams. telc also offers a separate telc Deutsch A2·B1 exam with its own format.

A1 · Start Deutsch 1

Written
Hören ≈20 min · Lesen + Schreiben 45 min

Sprechen
≈15 min, usually in a group of four, with no preparation time.

Short everyday texts and recordings, form filling, a short message, and simple questions and requests.

Official telc A1 format and practice test

A2 · Start Deutsch 2

Written
Hören ≈20 min · Lesen + Schreiben 50 min

Sprechen
≈15 min, usually in pairs, with no preparation time.

Practise extracting information from everyday conversations and texts and responding to written and spoken prompts.

Official telc A2 format and practice test

B1 · Zertifikat Deutsch

Written
Lesen + Sprachbausteine 90 min · Hören ≈30 min · Schreiben 30 min

Sprechen
≈15 min, usually in pairs, with 20 min preparation.

Sprachbausteine tests grammar and vocabulary in context. Writing has one task. Speaking includes getting to know your partner, discussing a topic, and planning together.

Official telc B1 format and practice test

Goethe-Zertifikat · Adult exams

A1 · Start Deutsch 1

Written
Hören ≈20 min · Lesen 25 min · Schreiben 20 min

Sprechen
15 min, in a group of up to four, with no preparation time.

Practise short messages, signs, everyday recordings, form filling, introductions, and simple requests.

Official Goethe A1 papers and audio

A2

Written
Hören ≈30 min · Lesen 30 min · Schreiben 30 min

Sprechen
≈15 min for two participants.

Write an SMS and an email. In speaking, exchange personal information, talk about your life, and plan an activity together.

Official Goethe A2 papers and audio

B1

Written
Hören ≈40 min · Lesen 65 min · Schreiben 60 min

Sprechen
15 min in pairs, with 15 min preparation.

Four separately assessed modules. Writing includes a personal email, an opinion post, and a short formal email. Speaking includes joint planning, a presentation, and follow-up questions.

Official Goethe B1 papers and audio
