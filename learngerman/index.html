HOW TO STUDY

Four ways to practise.

🎧 Listen first

Play the German dialogues, read along with the transcript, and repeat aloud.

🗣️ Speak out loud

Read a dialogue as both speakers. Compare your pronunciation with the lesson audio.

✍️ Write something

After each lesson, write two original sentences using the new words. Review them against the examples and grammar tip.
