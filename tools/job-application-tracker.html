Use it in three steps

  1. Download and open the CSV file in your spreadsheet app. Google Sheets users can choose File → Import → Upload.
  2. Delete the example row and add the jobs you are genuinely considering. Copy the job URL from the official listing.
  3. Review upcoming actions at a consistent interval. Update each status after you apply, hear back or close the opportunity.

Recommended status labels

Researching

Checking whether the requirements and location are a realistic match.

Ready to apply

CV and supporting details are prepared but not submitted.

Applied

Application submitted; record the exact date and next action.

Interview

Interview arranged or in progress; keep the date in your notes.

Closed

Rejected, withdrawn or no longer available; keep the record for learning.

A sustainable weekly routine

  • Monday: Look for relevant openings and prioritise a manageable shortlist.
  • Midweek: Apply thoughtfully and record all submitted applications.
  • Friday: Review upcoming interviews, deadlines and follow-up tasks.

A tracker improves organisation; it does not guarantee employment or interviews.
