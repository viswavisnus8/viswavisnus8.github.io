INSIDE THE BOOK

What you'll learn

  • Select job titles that match your actual skills and interests.
  • Improve CV wording without inventing experience or achievements.
  • Use practical AI prompts to analyse job descriptions and practise interviews.
  • Keep an application tracker so opportunities and follow-ups are not lost.

Who is this for?

Students, recent graduates and early-career job seekers—especially those navigating their first applications.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE / TRY IT YOURSELF

Try this: a truthful CV review prompt

Compare the job description below with my CV. Identify the five strongest matches and three areas I need to improve. Suggest clearer wording using only information I have provided. Do not invent achievements, employers, degrees, numbers or tools.

Illustrative exercise related to the guide; not a quotation from the PDF.

How to put it into practice

  1. Choose two or three target roles and collect genuine job descriptions.
  2. List real projects, skills and work achievements with evidence.
  3. Review your CV, then practise questions using your own examples.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before you read

Will AI write my entire CV?

It can help you structure and edit it, but you must check every fact and use only truthful experience.

Is this relevant to freshers?

Yes. The guidance focuses on turning coursework, projects, volunteering and genuine experience into clear examples.

Will this guarantee a job?

No. Hiring depends on role requirements, competition and employer decisions. The guide is for preparation.
