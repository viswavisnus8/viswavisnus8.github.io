INSIDE THE BOOK

What you'll learn

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE / TRY IT YOURSELF

Try this: turn a goal into a plan

I want to complete [goal] by [deadline]. I have [time available] and these constraints: [constraints]. Break the work into realistic steps with one clear deliverable per step. Flag assumptions and show how to check the result.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before you read

Do I need paid AI tools?

No specific paid subscription is required to apply the planning and review methods in this guide.

Can I paste company data into AI?

Only share information you are permitted to disclose. Avoid confidential, customer and personal information without appropriate safeguards.

Should I trust AI output immediately?

No. Verify facts, calculations, dates and decisions before relying on generated suggestions.
